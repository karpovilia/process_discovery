\section{Independent Structural Extension}

This extension contains 288 independent training datasets and 3168 audited calibration/validation episodes. There are 1 episodes violating the declared pre-crisis criterion; their process, mechanism, fit and episode are recorded in \texttt{calibration\_audits.json} and are retained in the results. The primary routing claims require complete pre-crisis coverage in their own stratum. Known and discovered structures share learned behavior and supplied resource maps. Complete method, action and component comparisons are in \texttt{analysis/full\_summary.csv}.
