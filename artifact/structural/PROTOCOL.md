# Structural campaign v2 — prospective extension, separate from frozen P0

## Scope and freeze
Six designed structures P1,P2,P3,P5,P4,P6, each four worlds (fixed, service, routing, combined) and 12 independent calibration datasets. All 288 fits remain in the registry. Known and discovered structure evaluated separately on the same datasets and test catalog. The primary deliverable requires at least P1/P2/P3 complete (144 fits). Equal weighting of designed structures does not represent a population of business processes. Technical development/pilot seeds are excluded from inference.

Generator specifications and normal scales are the six calibration/P*.json files. CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL.md records the truth-only selection rule and all inspected outcomes. Source, protocol and calibration hashes are sealed in FREEZE.json before v2 main fit 0. The preliminary v1 run was stopped after 56 accepted fits by a source-code audit, before inspection of main policy outcomes: its MAE/Brier selector differed from P0. All v1 outputs remain preserved as superseded implementation verification; v2 uses fresh independent streams and unchanged generator parameters. No replacements of difficult fits. Pre-crisis audit failures remain in the report; they cannot support claims about strictly pre-crisis calibration.

## Observation and execution contract
Lifecycle data include case admission, observed class and process ID, operation enable/start/complete times, activity and resource pool. Unique-case WIP counts admission minus completion; simultaneous operations do not multiply WIP. Finite FIFO resource pools, one unit per active operation, nonpreemptive lognormal service. Actions add one worker or halve future repeat probabilities at declared operational completion sites; prices 2 and 0.5 per unit of active time. Same action definitions, thresholds, planning arrivals and costs for all methods. Duration and repeat-effect denominators use original capacities even after adding a worker. Cost J uses the trapezoidal integral of sampled WIP plus active action charge, terminal WIP reported separately.

Supported executable subclass: block-structured sequences, XOR, AND split/join and binary visible-body loops, including nested loops and optional/silent redo. Unit-weight safe nets with at most 62 places,120 transitions,30 visible labels,32 pools. Pool mappings, capacities, action sites/effects and case attributes are supplied operational metadata in both variants; they are not claimed as discovered. The process tree alone is inferred in variant B using PM4Py Inductive Miner, noise threshold 0, completion-ordered training traces, no manual correction. Fitness, precision, reachable-marking checks and exact visible-language comparison (10,000-state bound) are saved. Added paths are structural errors, not silently repaired. Unsupported or nonexecutable discovery is a pipeline failure. Report success coverage and conditional costs separately; an explicit no-action operational fallback may be summarized additionally, never substituted silently.

## Behavioral calibration
Eight training episodes at [0.4,0.6,0.8,1.0] times safe rate, twice each; three validation episodes at [.5,.7,.9], arrivals40T, drain300T. Reference S,T and rates frozen independently. Features are intercept, observed complexity class, process ID, log(1+pool queue/original capacity), log(1+global WIP/S), log(1+process WIP/S). Duration uses log time, per-activity ridge alpha1, intercept in penalized design, empirical residual normal noise (minimum sigma .05), generated log durations bounded [-4,5]. Static models retain attributes but omit three load terms.

Routing target is the next enabled visible activity after an operation completes, or END; if several activities are enabled simultaneously, choose their lexicographically first label. Targets use future labels only as training labels. Features are taken at completion, before routing. Initial selection uses case attributes. Fit a per-source multinomial logistic regression C10 (600 iterations,tolerance1e-7), finite deterministic-class fallback. Load terms are only added at supplied repeat-intervention source labels; other route classifiers retain class/process context. In a net decision, candidate weight is the sum of predicted next-label probabilities over its silent-reachable first-visible labels. Binary loop repeat probability is normalized, bounded [.001,.95], then intervention multiplier applied. Fitted regressions/logistic laws differ from saturating rational true load laws. No true feedback coefficients enter fitting.

Duration selection gain is absolute Gaussian negative log-likelihood reduction for log time, threshold .01. Repeat selection gain is absolute multinomial cross-entropy reduction on repeat-source rows, threshold .005. These are the probabilistic loss families used in P0; MAE and Brier remain supplementary diagnostics. Strict > inequalities. Four global masks00/10/01/11, plus removal of each repeat source from mask11 when multiple sites exist. Save validation features/targets and coefficients. Validation WIP forecasts on the same observed arrival schedules provide diagnostics; they do not select components.

## Policies and planning
Each action at thresholds2S/4S/8S, plus no action. Require two successive half-T observations. Known-generator planner uses the true generator on the SAME planning schedules and policy catalog, without test arrivals. Planning/test regimes, horizons and reps are exactly CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL.md.

Per fit, all candidate-policy planning costs are saved for all component masks, each known/discovered structure, per-source ablations and known generator. Tie breaking is stable catalog order. Selected policies and their hashes are written to PREPARED.json before generating any test arrivals or outcomes. These receipts are immutable on resumption.

Feedback-strength heuristic: no simulation; estimate positive load-related increase in log duration between zero queue/WIP and q=2 times original pool capacity, WIP=4S (process WIP=4S for single process,2S for two). For each capacity action, score is the mean positive log-duration increase over its assigned activities divided by original capacity. For each rework action, score is the mean positive increase in repeat probability from the same two contexts, divided by max(.05,1-p_high). Repeat-target labels are inferred from training lifecycles: the next enabled label must already have occurred earlier in that case. Repeat probability sums fitted next-label probabilities over these observed targets, conditional on class/process; no true loop graph or generator coefficients enter the heuristic. Choose highest score (catalog tie break), activate at2S. This tests local feedback assessment, not a new optimized heuristic. Save scores. Report all fixed action/threshold baselines, so conclusions cannot rely solely on this heuristic.

## Independent test
48 conditions/repeats per fit: two baseline rates × three pulse conditions ×8 repeats, horizon180T. Use the true process for every policy, irrespective of planning structure. Save all policies' samples, completed-case times, schedules, counter seeds and metrics; no further selection. The same saved action outcomes evaluate all methods. Known-generator regret is signed paired difference in realized J, not guaranteed nonnegative per finite test. A hindsight test minimum is a separately labeled lower bound, never an oracle policy.

P2 diagnostic case mix .65 instead of .35, same policies and matched seed streams. P3 diagnostic actual rework reduction30%/70% (nominal50%), policies unchanged. These robustness sets are separate from main tests.

Metrics include J, J/no-action, generator-planner regret, action/policy agreement, pairwise ranking errors, severe-event rates, paired prevented events, observable trigger lead relative to no-action severe onset, calm and nonsevere activations, WIP integral, completed cycle mean/p95 with terminal censoring count, train/validation load support and discovery errors. P4 includes each process's WIP and completed cycle time. Severe onset is retrospective evaluation only; triggers never access it.

## Randomization and inference
Main seed base = 900000000000 + process_number*1000000000 + world_index*100000000 + training_seed*1000000. Training schedule/operations offsets10,000/20,000+episode*100. Validation30,000/40,000. Planning50,000/60,000+condition*100+repeat. Tests500,000/600,000. Technical pilot uses a separate base above150000000000. Each operation uses counter-based seed,case,activity,visit,draw-kind/channel; trajectories are paired across actions.

Independent units are the 12 trained datasets. Within each structure/world, first average over its paired test episodes, then compare per-fit differences; Student-t confidence intervals. Primary per-structure family: retained-load vs predictive selection under routing, known structure, Bonferroni6 two-sided intervals. Discovered structure and other mechanisms reported separately as secondary evidence; no pooling known/discovered as independent datasets. Equal-weight process summaries use normalized J/no-action and preserve per-process heterogeneity. Comparisons with heuristic and generator planner are explicit secondary estimates. No claim that all methods must win.

Selector sensitivity is diagnostic only: ΔR={0,.001,.0025,.005,.01} at ΔD=.01; ΔD={0,.0025,.005,.01,.02} at ΔR=.005. Reuse saved mask planning and test results. Report retention, action agreement, J, regret and severe frequency; do not select the best threshold using test costs.
