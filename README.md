A new frozen campaign completed 288 independent training datasets across six process structures and four feedback mechanisms. Under routing feedback, selected-discovered retained-load planning has mean normalized cost 0.542448 versus 0.627221 for predictive selection: a 13.5% reduction. The primary paired difference is -0.084773 with adjusted two-sided 97.5% interval [-0.135261, -0.034284]. The second primary contrast, against standard-IM structure under the same corrected executor, is -0.095822 [-0.120549, -0.071094]. Processes have equal weights; these are six designed classes, not a representative population of all business processes. Strong baselines and process-specific adverse findings remain in the papers.
